The Nigerian e-commerce sector is booming and projected to reach $22.9B by 2030. However, behind this growth lies a persistent challenge:
The “What I ordered vs what I got” problem, where customers receive products that do not match their expectations.

Over time, this creates a deeper issue than just bad purchases—it erodes trust, and once trust drops, repurchase intention declines.

My idea?
If platforms already display product reviews, we can go a step further by systematically comparing:
> What sellers claim (product descriptions and metadata)
> What customers actually experience (reviews and feedback)
> From this, we can:
    - Identify gaps between expectation and reality
    - Quantify product reliability
    - Generate a trust score with meaning, not just a number

The goal is not only to help customers make better decisions, but also to encourage seller transparency and accountability.
